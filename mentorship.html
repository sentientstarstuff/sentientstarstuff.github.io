Programming & Mentoring Initiatives

Workshop & Mentorship

Computational Astrophysics Research Preparation (CARP) Program

As the Founder and Director of CARP, I developed this training initiative during my NSF Postdoctoral Fellowship at UC San Diego. I organize and lead workshops tailored for community college students transferring to four-year universities to pursue undergraduate research.

The program equips transfer scholars with essential Python programming, data analysis skills, and personalized mentorship. Three cohorts have completed the program, with many securing competitive research internships.

Nationwide Access

Computational Research Access NEtwork (CRANE)

As a Core Leadership Team Member, I contribute to CRANE—a virtual nationwide data science training program led by graduate and postdoctoral researchers addressing high barriers to entry in computational research.

I develop curriculum materials and lead interactive workshops to provide a welcoming environment for underrepresented students, teaching fundamental computational tools and guiding early-career researchers toward data science paths.

Learn More About CRANE
Peer Mentorship

Physics & Astronomy Community Excellence (PACE)

As Co-Founder and Co-Lead during my graduate studies at UC Irvine, I helped establish PACE to provide structured peer-mentorship to incoming first-year graduate students.

The program supports incoming scholars through monthly professional development workshops and dedicated 1-on-1 peer mentoring to ease the transition into graduate school.

Learn More About PACE
Community Outreach

Additional Outreach & Mentorship Contributions

Beyond leading CARP, CRANE, and PACE, I actively contribute as a mentor and facilitator across several broader community initiatives:

  • STARTAstro: Summer research program providing research opportunities and career development for astrophysics students.
  • AstroReach SD: Mentorship program supporting high-school students seeking to be the first in their family to pursue a college degree.
  • CosmicTours: Portable planetarium initiative bringing space science directly to K-12 classrooms across San Diego.
