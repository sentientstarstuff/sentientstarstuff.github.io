Programming & Mentoring Initiatives

Computational Astrophysics Research Preparation (CARP) Program

CARPis a training and mentoring initiative that I developed as a National Science Foundation Astronomy & Astrophysics Postdoctoral Fellow at UC San Diego, focused on organizing interactive workshops for community college students who aspire to transfer to a four-year university and engage in undergraduate research.

The CARP program equips prospective transfer students with essential Python programming, data analysis skills, and personalized mentorship to streamline their transition to a four-year university. To date, three CARP cohorts have completed the program, with many scholars going on to transfer to four-year institutions and participate in competitive research and internship opportunities.

Computational Research Access NEtwork (CRANE)

CRANE is a virtual, nationwide data science training program led by a diverse team of graduate and postdoctoral researchers in physics and chemistry. CRANE addresses high barriers to entry in computational research that historically exclude underrepresented students.

As a CRANE leader, I develop curriculum materials, lead interactive workshops, and collaborate across institutions to:

  • Provide a welcoming environment for historically underrepresented students to learn and gain confidence in computational physics.
  • Equip students with fundamental computational tools and introduce advanced algorithms used in cutting-edge research.
  • Facilitate meaningful academic and professional networking opportunities.
  • Guide students taking their first steps toward careers in computational physics and data science.

Physics & Astronomy Community Excellence (PACE) Peer-Mentoring Program

PACE is a peer-mentoring program that I co-led with Dr. Francisco J. Mercado during my graduate studies at UC Irvine.

The program provides structured, formal mentorship to first-year graduate students through monthly professional development workshops and dedicated 1-on-1 mentoring to ease the transition into doctoral research.

In addition to these programs, I have also contributed to several other impactful initiatives, including the STARTAstro summer research program, the AstroReach SD program for high-school students seeking to be the first in their family to pursue a college degree, and the CosmicTours program bringing space science directly to K-12 students in San Diego with portable planetarium shows.
