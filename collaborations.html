Research Collaborations

C3VO Survey

The Charting Cluster Construction with VUDS and ORELSE (C3VO) survey maps five of the most massive overdensities in the early universe ($z \sim 2\text{--}5$). Combining Keck spectroscopy (DEIMOS/MOSFIRE) and VUDS with Voronoi Monte-Carlo environmental mapping, C3VO investigates how environmental density connects to star formation, AGN activity, galaxy color, and the dynamical virialization of assembling protoclusters across cosmic time.

Visit C3VO Homepage

GOGREEN Survey

The Gemini Observations of Galaxies in Rich Early Environments (GOGREEN) survey is a premier spectroscopic legacy project targeting 21 high-redshift ($z > 1$) galaxy clusters and massive groups. By securing over 1,000 galaxy spectra with Gemini, GOGREEN establishes the definitive benchmark for testing unconstrained theories of environmental quenching, tidal stripping, and structural evolution in distant cosmic calorimeters.

Visit GOGREEN Homepage

GRACE (Roman Program #2003)

GRACE is an approved ultra-deep wide-field grism spectroscopic survey with the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, designing a sensitive 3D map of $\sim 290,000$ emission-line galaxies across 1.5 square degrees in CDFS. Bridging the area-depth gap between JWST and Euclid, GRACE measures the pace of Epoch of Reionization ($z = 7\text{--}10$) Lyman-$\alpha$ emitters and uncovers how cosmic environments shape early galaxy growth.
