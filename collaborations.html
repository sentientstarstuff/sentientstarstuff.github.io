Research Collaborations

Observational cosmology and galaxy evolution thrive on international teamwork. My research leverages multi-wavelength dataset pipelines and state-of-the-art space and ground-based facilities across several multi-institutional survey collaborations targeting galaxies in a wide range of environments.

Survey Programs & Initiatives

High Redshift (z ~ 2–3) Keck & Subaru

C3VO Survey

The Clusters at z ~ 3 Very-Large-Scale Overdensity (C3VO) survey targets high-redshift galaxy structures to map the assembly of early galaxy clusters during peak cosmic star formation. By integrating optical/near-IR spectroscopy from Keck and Subaru, C3VO investigates early environmental quenching, protocluster dark matter halos, and the emergence of massive cluster galaxies.

Visit C3VO Homepage
Intermediate Redshift (1 < z < 1.5) Gemini Observatory

GOGREEN Survey

The Gemini Observations of Galaxies in Rich Early Environments (GOGREEN) survey is a multi-wavelength legacy program designed to study galaxy evolution across diverse cluster environments at 1.0 < z < 1.5. GOGREEN provides deep spectroscopic and photometric measurements to unpack how group and cluster density suppresses star formation and shapes the stellar mass function.

Visit GOGREEN Homepage
Roman Space Telescope Cycle 1 Approved

GRACE (Roman Program #2003)

GRACE is an approved NASA Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope Cycle 1 science project focusing on large-scale structure and galaxy clusters. Leveraging Roman’s panoramic wide-field infrared capabilities, GRACE aims to trace cosmic web filamentary structures, identify faint cluster populations, and quantify halo assembly bias with unprecedented spatial precision.
