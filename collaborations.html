Research Collaborations

Observational cosmology and galaxy evolution thrive on collaborative scientific networks. My research integrates observational data from multi-wavelength campaigns and state-of-the-art ground and space facilities to probe galaxy cluster formation, protoclusters, and environmental quenching across cosmic time.

Survey Programs & Initiatives

Keck & VUDS Overdensity Mapping

C3VO Survey

The Charting Cluster Construction with VUDS and ORELSE (C3VO) survey is an ongoing observational campaign designed to construct a nearly complete map of the five most significant overdensities identified in the VIMOS Ultra–Deep Survey (VUDS). C3VO integrates spectroscopy from DEIMOS and MOSFIRE on the Keck I/II telescopes alongside VUDS, utilizing a Voronoi Monte-Carlo density mapper originally developed within the ORELSE survey. Its Keck component targets star-forming galaxies down to $i_{\text{AB}} < 25.3$ ($\sim L^*_{\text{FUV}}$ at $z \sim 4.5$ and $< L^*_{\text{FUV}}$ at $z \sim 2.5$) and Lyman-$\alpha$ emitting galaxies to even fainter limits.

1 < z < 1.5 Gemini Observatory

GOGREEN Survey

The Gemini Observations of Galaxies in Rich Early Environments (GOGREEN) survey is a multi-wavelength legacy program designed to systematically examine galaxy evolution across group and cluster environments at $1.0 < z < 1.5$. GOGREEN delivers deep spectroscopic and photometric datasets to analyze environmental star-formation quenching, galaxy structural evolution, and the build-up of the cluster stellar mass function.

Roman Space Telescope Cycle 1 Approved

GRACE (Roman Program #2003)

GRACE is an approved NASA Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope Cycle 1 science program focused on galaxy clusters and large-scale cosmic structure. Leveraging Roman's wide-field infrared imaging capabilities, GRACE aims to map cosmic web filaments, trace low-mass cluster galaxy populations, and characterize halo assembly and structural evolution across cosmic time.
