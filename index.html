Welcome! I am an NSF Astronomy & Astrophysics Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Astronomy & Astrophysics at UC San Diego. My research focuses on unraveling the complex evolutionary pathways of massive galaxies and galaxy clusters across cosmic time.

By synthesizing deep space- and ground-based observations—from the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) to wide-field surveys—I investigate how massive galaxies assemble, shut down their star formation ("quench"), and evolve within the cosmic web. Beyond observational cosmology, I am passionate about creating equitable pathways into STEM through structured research preparation, peer mentorship, and public engagement.

Core Focus Areas

Observational Astrophysics

Investigating early galaxy quenching, protocluster assembly, and structural evolution using JWST, HST, and ground-based spectroscopic instruments.
